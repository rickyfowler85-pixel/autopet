# Behavioral Intelligence 02

This increment preserves the Vite, TypeScript, Canvas renderer, habitat composition, art, interaction surface, and passive-first philosophy. It replaces flat activity rolls with deterministic intentions and durable learning.

## Decision model

Every autonomous choice is selected in two stages. The creature first weighs an activity using temperament, hidden readiness, learned relative preference, confidence, and temporary activity habituation. It then weighs real opportunities that afford that activity. This prevents an activity from becoming common merely because the habitat exposes more objects for it.

An intention persists through `notice → approach → attempt → outcome → reflect`. Its target, deterministic route, timers, assistance context, and resolved outcome are saved. Reloading during a sequence therefore continues the same action and cannot learn twice from one attempt. Moving a target object during notice or approach deterministically updates the destination. Moving it during the attempt records a truthful assisted interruption rather than a success at an empty location.

Static habitat opportunities are modeled independently of rendering:

- Root shelter: rest and observe.
- Shallow pool edge: investigate, groom, and wade.
- Vegetation patches: forage, investigate, and groom.
- Open clearing: observe, roam, and play.
- Branch, stone, and seedpod: multiple object-specific affordances.

## Preference and habit learning

Activity preferences sum to one, as do object interests. They compete instead of accumulating independently. An outcome shifts its selected preference according to its quality relative to that activity's prior experienced outcome rate. Values retain interior minimums and maximums, so ordinary experience remains adaptable and every option remains possible.

Habituation is separate from preference. Repeating an opportunity or activity temporarily reduces its selection score. Its level decays from simulation time, restoring interest without random preference mutation. This supports specialization, changing interests, and stable tendencies for explainable reasons.

## Independence and evidence

Feeding and grooming track three distinct values:

- Capability grows slowly through repeated practice.
- Confidence follows experienced outcomes.
- Reliance rises with accepted assistance and falls through autonomous success. Absence alone never raises it.

Natural foraging and grooming are baseline autonomous activities. Hidden readiness helps the creature return to them reliably, but there are no player-facing meters, deadlines, damage, or failure from absence.

The latest 80 events remain available for immediate observation. Separate durable evidence summarizes activities, affordances, opportunities, assistance, duration, and outcomes. Up to 240 recent completed outcomes retain concrete subjects and resolved habitat positions for future Evolution Reflection, while aggregate summaries continue indefinitely.

## Determinism and persistence

Decision, action-outcome, and social-response randomness use separate persisted streams. Social interaction cannot accidentally perturb autonomous outcome order. Version-one local saves migrate their known position, object placement, visit counts, recent events, and current activity without inventing historical outcomes. Offline catch-up uses the same steps as live play and is capped at six hours; unrestricted advancement is reserved for deterministic development tests.

## Deliberate limits

This is a behavioral foundation. It does not implement visible care management, player food offerings, a memories interface, procedural anatomy, metamorphosis, or a fixed evolution tree. Temporary activity cues reuse the existing juvenile art rather than introducing a new production sprite set.
